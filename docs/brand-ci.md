# Trade The Future — amber color trial

User requested a live trial of the proposed CI palette.

- Main background: #101114 (charcoal)
- Cards: #1B1D22
- Raised surface: #22252B
- Brand/primary CTA: #F2B544 (amber)
- Highlight: #FFD58A
- Main text: #F5F2EB (warm white)
- Secondary text: #A7ABB4
- Dividers: #30343C

Gold is concentrated on identity, important headings and primary actions. Neutral borders replace the former olive/gold outlines; spotlight remains amber on hover. Original portrait and mascot colors preserved. Trial palette, not a finalized brand guide.
